# v0.1 campaign coverage matrix (static)

Generated by `scripts/audit-v01.mjs` from the 194 shipped maps
(`W/work/claude-s14-maps-001`, identical in 013/014/v0.1), one line per
original map with the per-alias maximum. Counts are static entity counts; the
last column says whether the map has been played at all. No v0.1 change has
been played yet.

Shared fixes applied to every map (no per-map edits in v0.1):
- Cutscenes: Simon actors drawn only in scene views (adapter); players packed
  with EF_NODRAW in scene cameras (014).
- Weapon grants: cof_giveitems weapon_* to every living player, owed to dead
  players until revived; duplicates prevented by an ownership check.
- Doors: converted co-op doors keep the fade/audio/teleport relocation;
  story-locked ones (master) now play the latch locked sound; key doors use the
  key-door bridge and the native locked sound. `-` = not applicable.
- Transitions: inventory carry read at ChangeLevel for every transition type.
- Recorders: host-only save with slot choice; partner notice at every recorder.
- Latches/endings: env_global story latches and shared ending flags are kept
  across maps and saved.

Unresolved (flagged): 21 natively disabled inter_doors stay silent when used
while disabled (single-player behavior); scripted triggers outside env_global
latches are not part of a save (a scene before a recorder can replay after a
load); non-Simon cutscene actors follow single-player visibility.
